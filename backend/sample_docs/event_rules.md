# Event Rules

## Hackathon
- **Eligibility:** Open to students in years 2 to 4.
- **Allowed Branches:** CSE, IT, AI&DS, ECE.
- **Team Size:** 2 to 4 members.
- **Fee:** ₹500 per team.
- **Rules:** Bring your own laptops. No external assistance allowed during the 24 hours.

## Coding Contest
- **Eligibility:** Open to all students (Years 1 to 4).
- **Allowed Branches:** All technical branches (CSE, IT, AI&DS, ECE, EEE, MECH, CIVIL).
- **Team Size:** Individual participation only (1 member).
- **Fee:** ₹100 per participant.
- **Rules:** No internet allowed. Permitted languages are C, C++, Java, and Python.

## Robotics Challenge
- **Eligibility:** Open to students in years 2 to 4.
- **Allowed Branches:** ECE, EEE, MECH, CSE, AI&DS.
- **Team Size:** 3 to 5 members.
- **Fee:** ₹750 per team.
- **Rules:** Robots must meet size restrictions (30x30x30 cm max). Autonomous operation only, no remote controls.

## AI Quiz
- **Eligibility:** Open to all students (Years 1 to 4).
- **Allowed Branches:** CSE, IT, AI&DS, ECE.
- **Team Size:** 1 to 2 members.
- **Fee:** ₹150 per team.
- **Rules:** No mobile phones allowed.
