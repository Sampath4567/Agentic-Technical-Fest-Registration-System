# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can first-year students participate in the Hackathon?**
A: No, the Hackathon is restricted to 2nd, 3rd, and 4th-year students.

**Q: What is the refund policy?**
A: Registration fees are non-refundable unless the event is cancelled by the organizers.

**Q: How does the mock payment work?**
A: Since this is a demonstration system, we do not process real payments. You just need to check the "I have completed the mock payment" box during registration to simulate a successful transaction.

**Q: Can I register for multiple events?**
A: Yes, as long as the schedules do not overlap.
